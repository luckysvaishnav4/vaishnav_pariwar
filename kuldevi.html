Kuldevi & family traditions

Kuldevi traditions can differ across families, regions and lineages. This page invites careful, sourced contributions without assuming one tradition belongs to everyone.

DEVOTION · MEMORY · FAMILY

Tradition carried
in many ways.

For many families, the Kuldevi tradition is a meaningful part of family identity and remembrance.

Practices and names can vary between branches of a family and across regions. We do not provide a universal directory or assume the same tradition is shared by all Vaishnav families.

How contributions are reviewed
A CAREFUL DIRECTORY

Share context, not assumptions.

CONTRIBUTE

Family knowledge

Describe which family branch or region a tradition relates to, when it is appropriate to share.

ADD A SOURCE

Make verification possible

Include a source or clearly label oral history and who has permission to share it.

KEEP IT CURRENT

Invite corrections

Let families suggest edits or request removal of contributed material.

Respectful contribution note

Family and temple contributions can include a source, region and family context. Send a sourced correction.
