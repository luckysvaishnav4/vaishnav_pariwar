Community guidelines

These practical guidelines explain how community submissions should be handled. They do not replace owner review or applicable law.

A FEW PRINCIPLES TO KEEP US GROUNDED

Respect the person.
Respect the source.

Community information is most useful when it is accurate, shared with permission and open to correction.

01

Share only what you may publish

Contribute text, images and documents you own or have permission to share. Get consent from people shown in photographs.

02

Keep personal details private

Do not submit another person’s phone number, address, identity information or profile without clear permission.

03

Provide sources and context

Distinguish confirmed announcements from examples, and oral history from documented records.

04

Expect review and correction

The site owner should moderate contributions, review information and remove material when appropriate.

Marriage profiles require specific safeguards.

A secure submission process, explicit public display consent, human review and a removal route must be in place before any real profile is published.

Community members should check this page against the site owner’s actual moderation and removal procedures before launch.
