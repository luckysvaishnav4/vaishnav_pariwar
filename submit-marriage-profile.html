Profile submission guidance

Use this guide to prepare for a thoughtful profile process, with consent and privacy at its center.

A PRIVATE DECISION, MADE BY YOU

Share your story
on your terms.

A thoughtful profile should reflect the person behind it—and stay under their control.

CONSENT
BEFORE
VISIBILITY

Prepare your information using the steps below. A profile can be shared when an owner-approved, secure submission route is available.

01

Choose what to include

Only share details you have reviewed and want displayed. Sensitive contact data should stay private.

02

Give clear consent

Public display needs explicit consent from the person featured, with a way to withdraw it.

03

Review before publishing

Moderators should confirm ownership, consent, accuracy and removal contact before listing.

A THOUGHTFUL NEXT STEP

Keep consent and privacy at the center.

Review the privacy guidance before sharing profile details through a community contact route.
