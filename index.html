A MORE CONSIDERED START

Good introductions
begin with respect.

A profile is a person’s story. It deserves to be shared only with their knowledge, approval and control.

How to share a profile
01

Choose what you share

Only provide information you’re comfortable discussing. Sensitive details do not belong in a public listing.

02

Get explicit permission

For a family member’s profile, get their informed consent before submitting anything about them.

03

Review before it goes live

Every introduction deserves time, thought and a personal review.
