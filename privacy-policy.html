What this site does today

This website is a static front-end with no submission service. Its forms do not transmit, save or publish submitted information. The scripts do not store profile or contact information in browser storage and no analytics tool is configured in this project.

Pages load Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons from jsDelivr. Images use Unsplash image URLs. Those external providers may receive standard connection information when a browser requests their files. Review their current privacy terms before public deployment.

If the site adds a backend

Before collecting profiles, contact details or photographs, the site owner must identify the data collected, the purpose, who can access it, where it is held, how long it is retained and how it can be deleted. Obtain informed consent before publication.

Your choices

A real community site should provide a clear way to view, correct or remove contributed material. Do not publish personal phone numbers, home addresses, identity documents or photographs without appropriate consent and safeguards.
